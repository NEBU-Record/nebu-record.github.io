Independent publisher and label · Lyon, France

Publishing for Brazilian funk and phonk songwriters

We sign and administer MCs, DJs and producers, take care of their publishing worldwide, and release music through our label, NEBU Records.

What we do

Registration

Each work gets declared with its writers, splits and ISWC, so streams can be matched to the song.

Collection

Performance and mechanical royalties, collected through SACEM internationally and The MLC in the United States.

NEBU Records

Our label releases our artists' tracks on all streaming platforms, with promotion built around each release.

Administration

Splits between co-writers, contracts in the writer's language, and clear royalty statements every period.
